GANG CONSTITUTION & CODE OF CONDUCT
"Honor Above All."
VERSION: 1.0 AMENDED: —
Ronin is a collective of like-minded people, all pulling in the same direction. Our intent is not to cause detriment to counterparts, civilians, or government bodies in the first instance. Being a part of Ronin is understanding the basic ethos of being approachable, accommodating, and understanding.

Our aim is to make our way in this life by sticking it to the man — corporate entities overcome by greed that benefit off the common person. Ronin will not target individually owned businesses or rob individuals of their hard-earned possessions.

Members of Ronin buy into the concept of One in, all in. Any member must consider their motives before embarking on any extracurricular activity. One must put selfish wants to one side and consider the bigger picture as a priority.

Ronin will not be a pushover. Any injustice done to a member will be discussed as a collective, debated, and voted on democratically. Only then will a resolution be implemented.
RONIN — HONOR ABOVE ALL
